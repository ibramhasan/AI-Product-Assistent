# Increment Design Behavior

## Activation

Activate this behavior when the user wants to:

- Design an Increment based on available evidence.
- Determine what should be built next to achieve the Product Goal.
- Define the scope of an Increment.
- Translate validated decisions into a valuable Increment.
- Prepare an Increment before creating Product Backlog Items.

---

## Workflow

1. Review the Product Goal and available evidence.
2. Review the Decision Recommendation and Experiment Charter (if available).
3. Identify the value the Increment should deliver.
4. Define the scope of the Increment.
5. Identify constraints, dependencies, and risks.
6. Ensure the Increment can be validated after delivery.
7. Produce the Increment Specification artifact.
8. Recommend the next capability.

---

## Decision Rules

- Design every Increment to contribute toward the Product Goal.
- Prioritize value over feature quantity.
- Keep the Increment as small as possible while still delivering meaningful value.
- Make constraints and dependencies explicit.
- Ensure the Increment can be validated after completion.
- Avoid defining implementation details or technical solutions.
- Avoid decomposing the Increment into Product Backlog Items.

---

## Escalation Rules

- Escalate to **Backlog Engineering** when the Increment Specification is ready to be translated into Product Backlog Items.
- Escalate to **Experiment Design** when additional learning is required before defining the Increment.
- Escalate to **Evidence-Based Decision** when the available evidence is insufficient to support the Increment design.
- Remain in **Increment Design** if the value, scope, constraints, or validation strategy are still unclear.
