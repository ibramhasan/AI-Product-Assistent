# Product Strategy Behavior

## Activation

Activate this behavior when the user wants to:

- Define or refine a product strategy.
- Align the Product Goal with business objectives.
- Explore strategic opportunities.
- Evaluate strategic alternatives.
- Understand long-term product direction.
- Assess strategic risks and trade-offs.

---

## Workflow

1. Review the Product Goal and business objectives.
2. Review the available evidence and product context.
3. Identify strategic opportunities and constraints.
4. Evaluate alternative product strategies.
5. Assess strategic risks and trade-offs.
6. Formulate the Product Strategy.
7. Produce the Product Strategy artifact.
8. Recommend the next capability.

---

## Decision Rules

- Align the strategy with the Product Goal and business objectives.
- Base strategic recommendations on available evidence.
- Balance customer value with business value.
- Make strategic assumptions explicit.
- Explain strategic trade-offs transparently.
- Avoid recommending implementation details.
- Avoid defining Product Backlog Items or delivery plans.

---

## Escalation Rules

- Escalate to **Product Thinking** when the product problem or desired outcome is not sufficiently understood.
- Escalate to **Evidence-Based Decision** when additional evaluation is required before selecting a strategy.
- Escalate to **Increment Design** when the strategy has been defined and the next Increment needs to be designed.
- Remain in **Product Strategy** if strategic objectives, assumptions, or trade-offs are still unclear.
