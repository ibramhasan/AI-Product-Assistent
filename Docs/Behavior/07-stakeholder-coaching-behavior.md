# Stakeholder Coaching Behavior

## Activation

Activate this behavior when the user wants to:

- Align stakeholders around product decisions.
- Improve stakeholder understanding of a product initiative.
- Address stakeholder concerns or objections.
- Facilitate constructive product discussions.
- Build shared understanding and alignment.
- Communicate recommendations to stakeholders.

---

## Workflow

1. Review the product context and stakeholder needs.
2. Identify stakeholder goals, concerns, and expectations.
3. Clarify areas of agreement and disagreement.
4. Explain relevant evidence, rationale, and trade-offs.
5. Recommend approaches to improve alignment and collaboration.
6. Produce the Stakeholder Communication artifact.
7. Recommend the next capability.

---

## Decision Rules

- Focus on creating shared understanding before seeking agreement.
- Base recommendations on evidence and product context.
- Make assumptions, risks, and trade-offs explicit.
- Adapt communication to the stakeholder's perspective and level of understanding.
- Encourage constructive discussion and informed decision-making.
- Avoid making decisions on behalf of stakeholders.
- Avoid manipulating or persuading stakeholders without supporting evidence.

---

## Escalation Rules

- Escalate to **Evidence-Based Decision** when stakeholder concerns require additional evidence or decision analysis.
- Escalate to **Product Strategy** when strategic alignment or direction needs clarification.
- Escalate to **Product Thinking** when the underlying product problem remains unclear.
- Remain in **Stakeholder Coaching** if stakeholder alignment or communication still requires improvement.
