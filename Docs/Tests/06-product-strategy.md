# Product Strategy Tests

This document defines the expected behavior of the Product Strategy capability.

---

# Happy Path

## PS-HP-001 — Define a Product Strategy

### Intent

Define a product strategy aligned with business objectives.

### User Input

Our company wants to expand into the small business market. Help us define a Product Strategy.

### Expected Capability

Product Strategy

### Expected Artifact

Product Strategy

### Expected Behavior

- Understand the business objectives.
- Identify the target customers.
- Define strategic outcomes.
- Align the Product Goal with business objectives.
- Produce a Product Strategy.
- Recommend the next capability.

### Should Not

- Create Product Backlog Items.
- Design an Increment.
- Build a roadmap with implementation details.

### Success Criteria

- Produces a Product Strategy.
- Aligns business objectives with product outcomes.
- Recommends the next capability.

### Failure Criteria

- Focuses on feature implementation.
- Produces delivery plans instead of strategy.

## PS-HP-002 — Evaluate Strategic Alternatives

### Intent

Compare multiple strategic options before selecting a direction.

### User Input

We can either expand internationally or deepen our existing domestic market. Help us evaluate these strategic options.

### Expected Capability

Product Strategy

### Expected Artifact

Product Strategy

### Expected Behavior

- Identify strategic alternatives.
- Evaluate benefits and trade-offs.
- Explain assumptions and risks.
- Produce a Product Strategy.

### Should Not

- Choose an option without analysis.
- Recommend implementation work.

### Success Criteria

- Evaluates alternatives objectively.
- Explains strategic trade-offs.
- Produces a Product Strategy.

### Failure Criteria

- Makes unsupported strategic recommendations.

## PS-HP-003 — Align Product Goal with Business Objectives

### Intent

Ensure the Product Goal supports the organization's strategic objectives.

### User Input

Our Product Goal is to improve onboarding, but our company objective is to increase customer retention. Help us determine whether these are aligned.

### Expected Capability

Product Strategy

### Expected Artifact

Product Strategy

### Expected Behavior

- Review the Product Goal.
- Review business objectives.
- Identify alignment gaps.
- Recommend strategic adjustments.
- Produce a Product Strategy.

### Should Not

- Redesign the Product Increment.
- Create Product Backlog Items.

### Success Criteria

- Clearly explains alignment.
- Produces a Product Strategy.

### Failure Criteria

- Ignores business objectives.
